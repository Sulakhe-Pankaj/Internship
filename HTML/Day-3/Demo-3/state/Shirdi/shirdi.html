SHIRDI

Shirdi became historically significant in the late 19th century due to Sai Baba, who lived there from around 1858 until his death in 1918. The village itself has older roots as a small agrarian settlement under the Ahmadnagar Sultanate and later the Maratha Empire. After 1818, it came under British rule. The Sai Baba movement transformed Shirdi into a major spiritual center, and the first temple dedicated to him was built in 1922. The town’s historical identity today is inseparable from Sai Baba’s teachings, his mosque Dwarkamai, the Chavadi procession tradition, and the Shree Sai Sansthan Trust, one of the richest temple organizations in India.
