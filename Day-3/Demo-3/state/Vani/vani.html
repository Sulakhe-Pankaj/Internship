Vani (Saptashrungi Gad)

Vani is historically important due to the Saptashrungi Temple, dedicated to Goddess Durga, located on Saptashrungi hill, one of the 51 Shakti Peeths in Hindu tradition. The region has ancient tribal and goddess worship roots predating organized temple structures. The temple gained prominence during the Maratha period when pilgrim access and infrastructure were expanded. The fortifications around the hill reflect its use as a protected religious site. Historically, Vani represents a blend of indigenous Devi worship, later medieval temple patronage, and Maratha religious administration.
