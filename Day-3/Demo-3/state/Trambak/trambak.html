Trambakeshwar

Trimbak’s history centers on the Trimbakeshwar Temple, one of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Shiva, built in the 18th century by Peshwa Balaji Baji Rao, though the site itself has ancient Shaivite worship origins. The town was historically under the Yadavas, then the Mughals, and later the Marathas. It is also the origin point of the Godavari river, which has given it religious and historical importance since early Hindu civilization. Trimbak historically symbolizes Maratha temple revival, sacred river geography, and ancient lingam-centered worship traditions.
